The _directory_ provides a catalog of prebuilt resources, allowing you to add ready-to-use components to your project and expand your agents' capabilities, for example, with [extension nodes](/ai/for-developers/extensions#extension-nodes) and [knowledge connectors](/ai/for-developers/extensions#knowledge-connectors).

Key advantages include:

- **Faster setup**. Install prebuilt resources instead of building them.
- **Flexibility**. Customize installed resources for your use case.
- **Reach**. Install or upload custom resources in your project to reuse.

## How to use

The directory provides prebuilt extensions and lets you upload custom extensions. On the **Extensions** tab, you can manage extensions that extend the functionality of your agents with extension nodes and knowledge connectors.

### Manage extensions

The **Extensions** tab displays all prebuilt or uploaded custom extensions as cards. The extension cards can include the following tags:

- `Nodes` — the extension includes an extension node.
- `Knowledge` — the extension includes a knowledge connector.
- `Installed` — the extension is installed.
- `Custom` — a custom extension that you uploaded.

Use the **Extension nodes** and **Knowledge connectors** toggles to display only extensions that include extension nodes or knowledge connectors.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Install extensions">

    1. On the **Extensions** tab, select **Extension nodes** or **Knowledge connectors**.
    2. Click the extension you want to install. The extension card expands with details.
    3. Click **Install**, then click **Install extension** to confirm that you accept the terms of the third-party providers.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Update or uninstall an extension">

    Extensions with a newer version show **Update available**. Open the extension, then click **Update** or **Uninstall**. In the dialog, confirm with **Update extension** or **Uninstall extension**.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Upload a custom extension">

    1. On the **Extensions** tab, click **+ Upload extension**.
    2. Drag your extension file into the dialog, or click **Browse to upload** and select the extension file from your device.
    3. Click **Upload**.

    To update a custom extension in your project, first upload the new version of the extension. After updating the extension, the custom extension card will display the **Update** button.

    To build your own extension, see [Custom extensions](/ai/for-developers/extensions#custom-extensions).

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
