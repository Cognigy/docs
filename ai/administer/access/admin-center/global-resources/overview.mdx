_Global Resources_ are Cognigy resources that you can assign to and share across different Projects in your organization. Global resources work similarly to their counterparts at Project level and have a similar [user interface as other resources](/ai/getting-started/navigate-the-platform#resource-pages).

Global resources include:

- [Global large language models](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/global-resources/global-llms)
- [Global connections](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/global-resources/global-connections)
- [Global speech providers](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/global-resources/global-speech-providers)
- [Global carriers](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/global-resources/global-carriers)

Global speech providers and carriers are available only if voice features are activated for your organization.

## Restrictions

- Only users with `admin` [global role](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/access-control) can access global resources.
- You can't export global resources as a [package](/ai/agents/deploy/packages) or [snapshot](/ai/agents/deploy/snapshots).

## Assign global resources to projects

A global speech provider or carrier is available only in the projects you assign it to.

1. Open the resource in the Admin Center.
2. In the **Assigned to Projects** section, click **Add projects**.
3. Select the projects. To select all projects that match your search, click **Select all**.
4. Click **Apply**, and then click **Save**.

To change the assignment, open the resource and click **Edit projects**.

<Warning>
  When you create a global resource, it has no assigned projects. Cognigy hides it from all projects until you assign it.
</Warning>

Global resources without a saved assignment show **All projects**.
