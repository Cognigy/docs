## Get set up in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access">
    Log in for the first time, then connect your identity provider. See [Access to Cognigy](/ai/administer/installation/access-to-cognigy-ai) and [single sign-on](/ai/administer/installation/single-sign-on-saml2).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Invite users and decide what each role can reach. See [Members](/ai/administer/access/members) and [Access control](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/access-control).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share resources">
    Configure an LLM or connection once, then assign it to projects. See [Global resources](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/global-resources/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and review">
    Track consumption and see who changed what. See [Admin Center](/ai/administer/access/admin-center/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>
