Cognigy.AI is organized around a few core screens:

- **Main page** — where you land after signing in and can view your [projects](/ai/agents/develop/projects-and-flows/projects).
- **Resource pages** — where you manage [flows](/ai/agents/develop/projects-and-flows/overview), [knowledge stores](/ai/agents/develop/knowledge), and other resources inside a project.
- **Navigation menu** — where you move between the pages of a project.

## Main page

<Frame>
  <img src="/_assets/ai/overview/user-interface/main-page.webp" alt="Cognigy main page with the projects sidebar, a greeting, recently opened projects, the activity feed, and product updates" style={{ width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

The main page is the landing page after you sign in. On it, you can manage your projects and see what changed in them recently.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Projects sidebar">
    In the **Projects** sidebar, you can find, open, and manage your projects. Pinned projects appear first, sorted by name. The other projects follow, with the most recently changed first. A colored dot next to a project shows its color.

    | Control | Description |
    |:-------:|-------------|
    | **Search projects** | Filters the list by project name. |
    | ![Pin projects sidebar](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/pin.svg) | Keeps the sidebar open. |
    | ![New project](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/plus.svg) | Opens the **New project** dialog, where you can [create a project](/ai/agents/develop/projects-and-flows/projects#create-project). |

    To manage a project, hover over it, click ![More options](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/ellipsis.svg), and select one of the following:

    - **Pin project** or **Unpin project** — Pins the project to the top of the list or removes it from there.
    - **Copy project ID** — Copies the project ID to your clipboard.
    - **Edit** — Opens the **Edit Project** dialog, where you can change the **Name** and **Color** of the project. Click **Save**.
    - **Delete** — Opens a confirmation dialog. Enter the project name and click **Delete project**. This permanently deletes the project and everything in it.

    **Edit** and **Delete** are available only if you have the required permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Top bar">
    | Button | Description |
    |:------:|-------------|
    | ![Toggle search bar](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/search.svg) | Opens the search bar. |
    | ![Documentation](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/book-open.svg) | Opens the [Cognigy documentation](https://docs.cognigy.com/) in a new tab. |
    | ![Toggle user menu](/_assets/icons/user-avatar.svg) | Opens the [user menu](/ai/administer/user-menu/overview), where you can open **Admin Center** (if you have access), open **My Profile**, log out, and see the release version. |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dashboard">
    | Section | Description |
    |---------|-------------|
    | **Recently opened** | Shows cards for the resources you opened most recently across your projects. Each card shows the resource type, the project color, and when you opened it. Click a card to open the resource. |
    | **Activity** | Lists recent resource changes across your projects, for example, who created or edited a flow. Click a row to open the resource, and click ![Refresh activity](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/refresh-cw.svg) to update the list. |
    | **Product Updates** | Shows a carousel of product news from your organization's updates feed. Click **Read more** to open the article, **All** to open all updates, or use the arrows and dots to browse. |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Navigation menu

<Frame>
  <img src="/_assets/ai/overview/user-interface/navigation-menu.webp" alt="Navigation menu with the project selector, the design, test, deploy, contact profiles, manage, and advanced groups, and the docs, products, and user menu entries" style={{ width: '40%' }} />
</Frame>

Inside a project, you use the navigation menu to move between the pages of that project.

At the top of the menu, the project selector shows the current project and lets you switch to another one, and ![Pin](/_assets/icons/black-and-white/pin.svg) keeps the menu open.

At the bottom of the menu, you can find:

- **Docs** — opens the [Cognigy documentation](https://docs.cognigy.com/).
- **Products** — opens other Cognigy products, for example, [Insights](/insights/overview).
- **User avatar** — shows your name and email. Click it to open the [user menu](/ai/administer/user-menu/overview), where you can open the admin center and [your profile](/ai/administer/user-menu/my-profile), or log out.
