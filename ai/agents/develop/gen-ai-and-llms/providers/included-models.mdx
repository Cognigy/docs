_Included models_ are LLMs and embedding models that NiCE Cognigy provides as part of your license. Their usage is included in the conversation price. You don't need to create a model or enter API credentials.

<Note>
  Before you can use included models, note the following:

  - You must sign a new SKU to enable included models.
  - Included models are available only to installations integrated with NiCE CXone.

  To set up included models for your organization, contact your customer success manager.
</Note>

## Activate included models

Models available in your region are activated by default. For models outside your region, an admin must activate them in the Admin Center under **Resources > LLMs**. Only users with admin role can activate included models.

To activate a model, follow these steps:

1. Go to the admin center and navigate to **Resources > LLMs**. The included models are listed in the **Included models** section.
2. Turn on the toggle for each model you want to use.

    - For a model with the **In region** tag, the toggle takes effect immediately. These models are activated by default.
    - For a model with the **Outside region** tag, a dialog asks you to confirm that you accept processing data outside your regional boundary. Select the checkbox and click **Accept & enable**. If your organization can't accept this, use your own model instead.

Once an admin activates a model, it's available in all projects in the organization.

To turn off an included model, switch off its toggle and click **Disable**. The model is no longer available in any project, so any resource that uses it stops working until you assign another model.

## Apply included models

To apply included models in a project, follow these steps:

1. In the navigation menu of the project, go to **Manage > AI & Voice Providers**. In **Language Models > Models**, you can identify the included models by the **Included** tag.
2. Go to **Language Models > Assignments** and select the included model next to the feature that should use it.
