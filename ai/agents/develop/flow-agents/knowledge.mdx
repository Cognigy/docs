A flow-based agent uses a **Knowledge Store** to answer from your documents instead of from the model's general training. You attach it in the **Knowledge Store** step when you [create the agent](/ai/agents/develop/flow-agents/overview#create-from-scratch), by choosing one of four options: **None**, **Choose existing Knowledge Store**, **Upload Knowledge Source**, or **Upload URL Knowledge Source**.

An embedding model is required either way. See [Knowledge AI](/ai/agents/develop/knowledge-ai/overview).
