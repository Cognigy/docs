_Voice_ lets your agents talk to callers over the phone. You create an agent, set how it speaks and listens, and connect it to a phone number. Cognigy handles speech recognition, conversation, and speech synthesis during the call.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your setup">
    Review what voice supports and check that your project is ready:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Explore technical specifications" icon="file-text" href="/ai/agents/develop/voice/technical-specifications">
        Review the supported protocols, audio codecs, trunk routing, speech providers, call recording, and call quality tools.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Configure speech and telephony" icon="user-cog" href="/ai/administer/access/speech-and-telephony">
        Ask your administrator to configure the speech providers and telephony connections for your project.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to build your voice agent">
    Decide whether to use agents or flows. Select a tab to see the voice features for each.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Agents" icon="bot">
        The following features are available only for agents:

        <CardGroup cols={2}>
          <Card title="Voice studio" icon="sliders-horizontal" href="/ai/agents/develop/voice-configuration#voice-studio">
            Set the speech providers, voice, greeting, turn taking, and listening options for an agent. Not available when speech to speech is on.
          </Card>
          <Card title="Voice tools" icon="wrench" href="/ai/agents/develop/voice-configuration#voice-tools">
            Let an agent hang up, transfer, or play audio during a call.
          </Card>
          <Card title="Speech to speech" icon="audio-lines" href="/ai/agents/develop/voice/speech-to-speech">
            Experimental. Run a real-time voice model for natural conversation, backed by your existing agent. Replaces the text-to-speech and speech-to-text settings.
          </Card>
        </CardGroup>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Flows" icon="workflow">
        The following features are available only for flows:

        <CardGroup cols={2}>
          <Card title="Voice nodes" icon="network" href="/ai/agents/develop/node-reference/voice/voice-gateway/overview">
            Build call logic with voice nodes, for example, to set session parameters, collect keypad input, play audio, or transfer the call.
          </Card>
        </CardGroup>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try additional features

The following features work with agents and flows:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Outbound calls" icon="phone-outgoing" href="/ai/agents/develop/voice/outbound-calls">
    Start calls from Cognigy to a phone number or another system, and track call statuses through webhooks and call events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="TTS streaming" icon="bolt" href="/ai/agents/develop/voice/tts-streaming">
    Play synthesized speech as it's generated to reduce response latency.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Click To Call" icon="mouse-pointer-click" href="/click-to-call/overview">
    Embed a voice widget in your website so that customers can speak directly with agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Voice endpoint" icon="radio-tower" href="/ai/agents/deploy/endpoint-reference/voice-gateway">
    Connect an agent to your contact center or other phone numbers with the Voice endpoint.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
