  ## Cognigy 2026.24 is out! <span className="cgy-rn-title-tag">Latest</span>

  **Released** November 24, 2026

  Introduces premium models, new OpenAI and Azure OpenAI models, and the experimental Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy) endpoint. This release also removes Nuance as a TTS and STT vendor.

<Update label="2026.23">
  ## Cognigy 2026.23

  <a href="/release-notes/2026.23" className="cgy-rn-cover-link"><img src="/_assets/release-notes/2026.23-launch.svg" alt="Cognigy 2026.23" className="cgy-rn-cover" loading="lazy" /></a>

  **Released** November 11, 2026

  Introduces the next generation of the platform: the Agentic Experience Studio, next-generation agents, AI Assist, voice-to-voice support, agent tracing, and multivariate testing. This release also includes: Models Included with Your License and Google Gemini 3.8 Flash Support.
